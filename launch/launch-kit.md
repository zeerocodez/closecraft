> For service businesses, revenue is rarely lost at the point of lead acquisition—it is lost in the follow-up. Inbound enquiries from Facebook Ads, web forms, and WhatsApp chats often sit in chaotic spreadsheets or unmonitored group chats, while busy teams wonder who was supposed to call back.
>
> Closecraft solves the follow-up breakdown:
> 1. **Instant Multi-Channel Ingestion:** Capture leads from web forms, Facebook Ads, or bulk CSV uploads into a single shared inbox in seconds.
> 2. **Clear Ownership & Today Radar:** Every viable enquiry is assigned to a specific team member with an active SLA timer so no thread is forgotten.
> 3. **Explainable AI Qualification:** Filter high-intent buyers by budget and timeline so closers speak with serious prospects first.
> 4. **1-Click WhatsApp Outreach:** Launch pre-formatted, compliant WhatsApp conversations directly without manual number copy-pasting.
> 5. **Dedicated Sales Closers On-Demand:** Attach vetted, certified remote sales professionals from the Digital Sales School (DSS) on 30-day managed sales pilots without hiring overhead.
> 6. **Bachs.io Payment Settlement:** Settle client deposits and subscription renewals seamlessly via dynamic bank transfers, cards, and mobile money.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (Launch-Ready)

**Q: How does the 14-day free trial work?**  
A: You receive full access to all Starter features for up to 3 team members. No credit card or payment commitment is required. At the end of 14 days, you can choose to renew manually for ₦20,000/month or upgrade to Pro.

**Q: What is the difference between Starter (₦20,000) and Pro (₦45,000)?**  
A: Starter includes 3 internal team seats—ideal for lean agencies establishing structured follow-up routines. Pro expands capacity to 10 internal team seats and unlocks automated round-robin lead routing, manager call coaching queues, and priority notification dispatch.

**Q: How are payments handled?**  
A: Payments are processed via Bachs.io, the official payment gateway for Nigerian businesses. You can renew manually using dynamic bank transfers, debit cards (Mastercard, Visa, Verve), or mobile money. Renewals are manual; we do not auto-debit your accounts.

**Q: Do remote sales closers consume my internal seats?**  
A: No. Remote professionals attached to your workspace through 30-day Managed Sales Pilots have dedicated external access and never count against your 3-seat or 10-seat internal quota.
