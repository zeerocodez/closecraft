## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an operational revenue command environment engineered for high-velocity revenue teams, CROs, and deal executives. The visual doctrine balances machine-speed automation with senior human judgment. Rather than positioning artificial intelligence as an autonomous sci-fi agent, the aesthetic models AI as an analytical co-pilot and real-time operational engine: dependable, auditable, structured, and fast.

The interface prioritizes dense, high-clarity data communication over decorative whitespace or marketing embellishments. It repudiates common SaaS tropes—such as floating gradient orbs, blurry glassmorphism, chatbot-first conversations, and decorative empty-state illustrations—in favor of crisp structural divisions, tabular operational ledgers, and unmistakable visual hierarchy. Every visual artifact must convey commercial intent, velocity, risk, or opportunity status with zero latency and absolute legibility.

## Colors

The palette operates under a high-contrast corporate paradigm that uses dark structural anchoring for executive navigation paired with razor-sharp light operational canvas tiers.

- **Primary Brand & Interactive Accent (`#4F7CFF`)**: Electric Blue powers primary calls-to-action, active operational triggers, focal outlines, and definitive routing actions. Subdued tints (`#EEF3FF`, `#E0EBFF`) serve as hover fills and selected row highlights.
- **Deep Anchors (`#0C1424`, `#111C30`)**: Used for the global command bar, left execution rail, and critical metric banners to convey enterprise authority and focus.
- **Canvas & Card Layers (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F5F1E8`)**: Core operational views render on `#F8FAFC` with active data modules anchored on pure `#FFFFFF`. `#F5F1E8` (warm off-white) acts as an intentional contrast floor for executive briefing strips and deal qualification summaries.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E2E8F0`, `#CBD5E1`)**: Crisp, low-tolerance structural separators replacing decorative shadows.
- **Semantics**:
  - **Success / Closed Won (`#059669`)**: Emerald tone with `#ECFDF5` background and `#A7F3D0` stroke.
  - **Attention / Pipeline Stall (`#D97706`)**: Amber tone with `#FFFBEB` background and `#FDE68A` stroke.
  - **Risk / Deal Drop (`#DC2626`)**: Crimson tone with `#FEF2F2` background and `#FECACA` stroke.
  - **AI Automation & Confidence Engine (`#6366F1`)**: Grounded violet/indigo tone with `#EEF2FF` background and `#C7D2FE` stroke. Communicates model certainty and machine routing without neon artifacts.

Status must never be communicated using color alone. Every status representation requires a paired semantic icon, a machine-readable text badge, and explicit confidence scores or delta labels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements Plus Jakarta Sans for executive headlines and section framing, coupled with Inter for dense transactional data, pipeline tables, and functional controls.

All numerical representations—including pipeline values, win probabilities, conversion rates, and SLA timestamps—must explicitly enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to ensure exact column alignment across fluctuating values. Micro-copy and data table headers use `label-caps` in uppercase styling with deliberate tracking (`0.06em`) to ensure legibility across multi-tenant dashboards on mobile screens.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (`min-width: 1024px`) shifting to an 8-column layout on tablet (`min-width: 640px`) and a 4-column compact stack on mobile screens (`max-width: 639px`). 

Information density must remain high while safeguarding touch targets (`44px` minimum vertical height on mobile viewports). 

- **Desktop Layout**: Dedicated 64px collapsed operational execution rail, fluid primary workspace accommodating split ledger views (deal queue list left 40%, AI activity and deal orchestration right 60%).
- **Mobile Reflow**: Workflows switch from split-pane to an interactive bottom-docked navigation paradigm. Priority deal metrics collapse into horizontal swipeable strips with pinned contextual action bars at screen bottom. Gaps between ledger rows default to `space-xs` (4px) with inner row padding locked at `space-md` (12px) to maximize viewable data rows per screen height.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual separation relies on calibrated surface borders (`#E2E8F0`, `#CBD5E1`) and structured tonal layering rather than heavy ambient blurs.

- **Level 0 (Base Surface)**: `#F8FAFC`. Flat ground layer for screen viewports.
- **Level 1 (Data Cards, Ledgers, Metric Blocks)**: `#FFFFFF` encased in a 1px solid stroke of `#E2E8F0`. Zero ambient blur in rest state. 
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Rails, Command Drawers)**: `#FFFFFF` surrounded by a 1px solid border `#CBD5E1` and a disciplined directional drop shadow: `0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(12, 20, 36, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Execution Sheets, Routing Dialogs)**: 1px border of `#0C1424` at 12% opacity with an ambient shadow of `0px 16px 32px -4px rgba(12, 20, 36, 0.16)`.
- **Active Focus & Pipeline Selections**: Direct replacement of the border stroke with `#4F7CFF` (Electric Blue) at 1.5px width and an outer ring `0 0 0 2px #E0EBFF`.

## Shapes

The system enforces roundedness level `1` (Soft: 0.25rem / 4px base radius) to establish an operational, engineered aesthetic. 

- Form inputs, standard buttons, data table cells, and deal tags adhere to `4px` corner radii.
- Composite command cards, modular pipeline widgets, and popovers scale to `rounded-lg` (`8px`).
- Modals, mobile bottom sheets, and major viewport containers use `rounded-xl` (`12px`).
- Circular indicators (`rounded-full`) are strictly preserved for status pips, user avatars, and machine-state indicators. Overly soft pill-shaped action buttons are avoided.

## Components

### Buttons & Operational Triggers
- **Primary Execution Button**: `#4F7CFF` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` radius, `0 1px 2px rgba(12, 20, 36, 0.1)` shadow. Hover switches to `#3B68E9`. Press state introduces inset tone.
- **Secondary Command Button**: `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, `#0F172A` text.
- **Destructive Action**: `#FEF2F2` background, 1px border `#FECACA`, `#DC2626` text. Hover transitions to `#FEE2E2`.
- **Human Approval Action**: `#0C1424` background, `#F5F1E8` text, accompanied by an explicit shortcut badge (`⌘ + Enter`).

### Data Ledger & Opportunity Cards
- Rendered as compact, border-separated blocks.
- **Top Row**: Lead name, account domain, ARR badge (tabular numerals, bold), and opportunity stage badge.
- **Middle Row**: AI Qualification summary (maximum 2 lines, truncated with hover reveal) styled with an indigo co-pilot indicator.
- **Action Strip**: Direct inline quick-actions: `Approve Route`, `Re-qualify`, `Assign Rep`.

### AI Confidence & Routing Badges
- Encased in a `4px` border radius with `#EEF2FF` fill and `#C7D2FE` border.
- Structure: A discrete 12px structural icon, clear semantic text ("AI Qualified - High Velocity"), and an explicit numerical confidence rating ("94% match"). Never show generative sparkles or futuristic glow shaders.

### Inputs & Qualification Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: 1px `#CBD5E1`.
- Typography: `14px` (`body-md`) with high-contrast text `#0F172A`.
- Active State: Border `#4F7CFF` with a 2px outer aura `#E0EBFF`. No layout shift.
- Inline validation displays immediate contextual icons (check or alert triangle) alongside exact resolution messaging.

### Checkboxes, Radios, and Toggles
- Checkboxes: 16x16px with a `3px` corner radius. Off state uses `#CBD5E1` border; checked state transitions immediately to `#4F7CFF` fill with a crisp white tick.
- Toggles: 36x20px enclosure with an internal 16px white disk. Transition uses an instantaneous mechanical timing function (`150ms ease-out`).
