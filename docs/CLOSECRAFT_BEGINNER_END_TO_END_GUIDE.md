# The Simple, Complete Guide to CloseCraft
### *How to Turn Strangers into Happy, Paying Customers (Even If You Know Nothing About Tech!)*

---

## Welcome! What is CloseCraft?

Imagine you own a busy shop in town. 

Every day, people walk past your shop. Some people look at your window. Some people walk in and ask: *"How much is this?"* 

Now, imagine three bad things happen:
1. **You are busy**, so you take three hours to answer them. By then, they have walked to another shop and bought from someone else.
2. **You write names on paper napkins**, and by Friday, the napkins are lost. You forgot who wanted to buy from you!
3. **A customer pays you**, but your worker forgets to wrap their box and deliver it. The customer gets angry.

**CloseCraft fixes all of this.**

Think of CloseCraft like a super-smart shop assistant who never sleeps, never forgets a name, and does four simple things:
1. Catches people who ask about your work on Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, or your website.
2. Tells you who is ready to pay right now and who is just looking around.
3. Helps your team send quick, polite messages on WhatsApp in one click.
4. Makes sure you deliver what you promised so your customers stay happy.

Let us walk through how you use CloseCraft from day one, step by step!

## Step 1: How People First Find You (Introductory Offers)

Before someone buys a big service (like a ₦1,000,000 project or a big contract), they usually want a small taste first. In CloseCraft, we call this an **Introductory Offer**.

### What does this look like?
- It could be a **Free 15-Minute WhatsApp Audit** of their problem.
- It could be a **₦50,000 Quick Review** or Starter Diagnostic.
- It could be a short **Video Demo (VSL)** on your website explaining how you help them.

### Where do these people come from?
People see your offer in three places:
1. **Your Website:** They visit your site, see your video, and type their name and phone number.
2. **Facebook or Instagram Ads (Meta Lead Ads):** They see your post on Instagram, click *"Learn More"*, and fill out a quick box on their phone with their name and phone number.
3. **Direct WhatsApp Messages:** A friend gives them your number, and they text you asking for help.

> [!NOTE]
> **The Good News:** You do not have to copy and paste anything! CloseCraft catches them automatically the second they click submit.

## Step 2: The Smart AI Brain Checks the Lead

As soon as a new person touches your business, CloseCraft’s smart brain wakes up.

In business, a new person is called a **Lead**.

CloseCraft checks three things in two seconds:
1. **Did they give a real phone number and email?** 
   *(CloseCraft cleans the number so it works on WhatsApp, like turning `0803...` into `+234803...`)*.
2. **How much money do they want to spend?**
3. **Are they ready to buy today, or are they just curious?**

### The Intent Score (The Flame 🔥)
CloseCraft gives every person a score from **1% to 100%**:
- **Cold (Below 50%):** Just looking around. Maybe they want free tips.
- **Warm (50% to 69%):** They have a real problem and want to know your price.
- **Hot (70% and above 🔥):** They have money in hand, need it fixed this week, and want to talk to someone right now!

### The Next Best Action (Your Cheat Code)
You do not have to guess what to do. CloseCraft puts a bright green box next to the customer that says:
- **"Next Best Action: Send Scoped Quote"**
- Or **"Next Best Action: Book a 15-Minute Call"**
- Or **"Next Best Action: Assign Human Closer"**

It tells you *who* should do it (the AI, an automation, or a real human on your team) and *why*.

CloseCraft puts all messages into one screen called the **Unified Inbox** (`/inbox`).

Here is how you use this room:

1. **Pick a person on the left:** Click on their name.
2. **Look at the middle:** You can read everything they said.
3. **Assign a teammate:** At the top, there is a little box called **Rep**. Click it and pick who on your team should handle this customer (for example, *Zainab* or *Emmanuel*).
4. **Use a 1-Click Template:** You do not even have to type! Click one of the buttons:
   - Click `Book Demo 📅` &rarr; CloseCraft types a polite invite for a call.

5. **Send it on WhatsApp:** Click **"Dispatch via WhatsApp"**. CloseCraft opens WhatsApp directly to that person with the message already written! All you do is hit send.

## Step 4: The Deal Pipeline (Moving from "Hello" to "Paid!")

When someone says: *"Yes, I am interested, tell me how we can work together"*, they become a **Deal**.

A Deal lives in your **Deal Pipeline** (`/pipeline`).

Think of the Pipeline like a train track with 5 stations:

```
[ STATION 1 ]    →    [ STATION 2 ]    →    [ STATION 3 ]    →    [ STATION 4 ]
OPPORTUNITY            PROPOSAL              NEGOTIATION           WON (PAID! 💰)
Someone wants         You sent them         They want a small     They paid you!
your service          the price list        discount or contract  Money in the bank.
```

### How to use it:
1. You see cards on your screen, like a card for **"Dr. Ifeanyi Uzoma - ₦2,200,000"**.
2. **Click on the card!** A big sheet opens showing you their phone number, what they need, and their notes.
3. When you send them your price, click the button that says **"To proposal"**.
4. When they agree and pay your bank account, click **"Won"**!
5. Instantly, your dashboard counts the money, congratulates your closer, and marks the deal as Closed-Won.

## Step 5: The Promise Ledger (Delivering What You Sold)

Many businesses fail because they take a customer's money and then take four weeks to deliver. 

CloseCraft has a special tool called the **Promise Ledger** (`/commitments`).

Whenever you tell a customer on WhatsApp:
> *"I will send you the design draft by Thursday 4 PM."*

CloseCraft spots that promise. It creates a timer with a clock.
- If you have 2 days left, it stays green.
- If you have 2 hours left, it turns yellow.
- If you are late, it turns red and alerts your manager: *"Warning! You promised Chief Adeleke his file today. Please finish it now!"*

This ensures nobody ever calls your business unreliable.

## Step 6: The Lead Recovery Hub (Finding Free Money You Forgot)

What happens to people who asked for your price two months ago and then stopped replying? Most businesses throw them away and spend more money on new ads.

CloseCraft does not do that. It has the **Lead Recovery Hub** (`/recovery`).

1. It searches your old leads who went silent 30, 60, or 90 days ago.
2. It groups them together into a list.
3. It creates a friendly, polite check-in message:  
   *"Hi John, I know you were looking to fix your sales operations a few weeks back. Did you ever get that sorted, or would you still like a quick hand?"*
4. You click **"Dispatch Recovery Campaign"**.
5. Suddenly, 5 or 10 old leads write back: *"Oh thank you for checking on me! Yes, I was busy, let us do it now!"*  
   **You just made sales without spending a single Naira or Dollar on new ads.**

## Step 7: The AI Practice Coach (Training Your Team to Close)

What if you or your sales workers are shy, or don't know how to answer angry questions like *"Your price is too high!"*?

CloseCraft includes the **Practice Studio** (`/practice`).

1. Click on **"AI Practice Coach"** on the left menu.
2. Click **"Start This Exercise"**.
3. A pretend customer appears on the screen (for example, a busy company boss named Mr. Tunde).
4. Mr. Tunde says: *"I don't have time for this, and your fee is double what others charge. Why should I use you?"*
5. You type your answer.
6. The AI acts like a real coach:
   - It tells you: *"Good job staying polite!"*
   - It tells you: *"Next time, ask him what his problem is costing him before talking about your fee."*
   - It gives you a grade score (like 85/100).

Your team can practice here every week until they are confident closers!

## Step 8: Connecting Your Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

If you run ads on Facebook or Instagram, you want every lead who clicks your ad to land inside CloseCraft instantly.

### Where do you set this up?
1. Click **SETTINGS** at the bottom of the left menu.
2. Click **Meta Lead Ads** (or go to `/settings#meta-ads`).
3. Click the blue button: **"Connect Facebook & Instagram"**.
4. Log into your Facebook account and give CloseCraft permission.
5. CloseCraft will automatically find your Facebook Page and your Lead Forms.
6. Turn the switch to **Active**.

From that moment on:
- A customer sees your Instagram ad at 11:00 PM while lying in bed.
- They tap *"Sign Up"*.
- At 11:00 PM and 2 seconds, their name, phone number, and message are sitting in your **CloseCraft Inbox**.
- CloseCraft scores them, tells you what to do, and tracks every single Naira or Dollar that came from that exact ad!

## Summary: Your Daily 10-Minute Morning Routine

Every morning when you wake up, here is all you need to do:

1. **Open the Inbox (`/inbox`):**
   - Look at the new messages from the night before.
   - Reply to the Hot leads (🔥) first using your 1-click WhatsApp buttons.
   - Assign leads to your sales reps.
2. **Open the Pipeline (`/pipeline`):**
   - Check who received a proposal yesterday. Call them or text them.
   - Move paid customers to **Won**.
3. **Open the Command Centre (`/dashboard`):**
   - See how much money your business made this week.
   - See which ads worked best.

**That is all! You do not need to be a programmer or a computer genius. CloseCraft takes care of the technical heavy lifting, while you and your team focus on smiling, talking to people, and closing deals.**
